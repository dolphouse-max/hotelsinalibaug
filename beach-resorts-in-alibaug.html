Best Beach Resorts in Alibaug for a Relaxing Weekend

Alibaug is one of the most popular beach destinations near Mumbai. Visitors looking for coastal vacations often search for beach resorts located close to Nagaon Beach, Kashid Beach and Alibaug Beach.

Why Choose Beach Resorts in Alibaug?

Many travelers choose beach resorts in Alibaug because they provide direct access to the beach, peaceful surroundings and beautiful sea views.

Popular areas for beach resorts include Nagaon Beach, Kashid Beach and Alibaug Beach.
