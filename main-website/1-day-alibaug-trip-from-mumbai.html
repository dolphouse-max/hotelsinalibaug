1 Day Alibaug Trip from Mumbai

Alibaug is one of the most popular day-trip destinations near Mumbai. Located along the Konkan coast of Maharashtra, the town is known for its beaches, seafood restaurants and relaxed seaside atmosphere. Many travelers visit Alibaug for a quick one day trip to enjoy beach views, coastal food and scenic ferry journeys.

A one-day trip works best when you keep the plan simple. In most cases, trying to cover too many beaches reduces the experience, while choosing one main beach zone and one sightseeing stop usually makes the day feel much better.

How to Reach Alibaug from Mumbai

The distance between Mumbai and Alibaug is about 95–100 kilometers. Travelers can reach Alibaug either by ferry or by road.

  • Ferry: Ferries from Gateway of India to Mandwa Jetty take around 45–60 minutes.
  • Road: Driving from Mumbai via Panvel and Pen takes around 2.5 to 3 hours.

For a one day trip, many visitors prefer taking the ferry because it saves travel time and offers beautiful sea views during the journey.

If your day starts early, the ferry route usually gives the cleanest format for a quick Mumbai-to-Alibaug outing. Road travel can still work, especially if you want more flexibility around return timing.

Suggested 1 Day Itinerary for Alibaug

If you are planning a quick one day trip from Mumbai, the following itinerary works well.

The key is to avoid overloading the day. One beach, one meal and one extra stop are usually enough for a satisfying short trip.

Morning

  • Take the early morning ferry from Mumbai to Mandwa.
  • Travel to Nagaon Beach or Alibaug Beach.
  • Enjoy breakfast at a beachside restaurant.

Afternoon

  • Explore Kolaba Fort near Alibaug Beach.
  • Try water sports at Nagaon Beach.
  • Enjoy seafood lunch at a coastal restaurant.

Evening

  • Watch sunset along the beach.
  • Return to Mumbai by evening ferry or road.

Best Beaches for a Day Trip

For a short trip, it is best to focus on one or two beaches instead of visiting many places.

  • Nagaon Beach – Known for water sports and restaurants
  • Alibaug Beach – Famous for views of Kolaba Fort
  • Kashid Beach – Known for white sand and scenic coastline

Among these, Nagaon Beach is one of the most popular choices for day visitors because it offers a wide shoreline and many beach activities.

Alibaug Beach is better if you want easier access to Kolaba Fort and the town side. Kashid is beautiful, but it usually fits better into a longer trip than a very short one-day outing.
