7 Hidden Beaches in Alibaug Most Tourists Miss

While Alibaug is famous for beaches like Nagaon and Alibaug Beach, the region also has several quieter coastal spots that many tourists miss. These hidden beaches offer peaceful views, fewer crowds and beautiful natural surroundings for travelers who want a more relaxed beach experience.

These beaches are usually better for slow travel than for activity-heavy plans. If your idea of a good Alibaug trip includes quiet walks, cleaner air, fewer commercial operators and longer scenic pauses, the lesser-known beaches often feel more rewarding than the busiest main stretches.

1. Awas Beach

Awas Beach is a peaceful coastal stretch located near Alibaug town. Compared to more popular beaches, Awas Beach is usually quieter and surrounded by greenery. It is ideal for long beach walks and sunset views.

Awas suits travelers who want a calmer version of the Alibaug coastline without going too far from the main circuit. It works well for couples, quiet stays and short detours during a broader beach-hopping day.

2. Revdanda Beach

Revdanda Beach is located near the historic Revdanda Fort. The beach is wide and less crowded, making it a great location for travelers looking for a quiet coastal escape.

This beach is particularly useful for travelers who want to combine scenery and history in one outing, since the Revdanda side gives you a more layered coastal experience than a simple beach stop.

3. Kihim Beach

Kihim Beach is known for its calm environment and natural surroundings. It is also popular among bird watchers and nature lovers.

Kihim is one of the better picks for travelers who care about trees, quieter shoreline atmosphere and a more nature-led stop rather than a busy swimming or activity beach.

4. Varsoli Beach

Varsoli Beach is located close to Alibaug town but still remains less crowded than the main beach. The calm shoreline makes it a pleasant place for morning walks.

Its location makes it useful for travelers who want something quieter without committing to a longer drive away from town facilities.

5. Akshi Beach

Akshi Beach is a scenic beach located between Alibaug and Nagaon. It is known for its long sandy coastline and relatively quiet environment.

Akshi is a good compromise choice for travelers who want a beach that feels more open and calmer than the busiest spots but still sits within the main Alibaug-Nagaon movement zone.

6. Thal Beach

Thal Beach is another peaceful coastal location in the Alibaug region. Visitors often enjoy its relaxed atmosphere and scenic surroundings.

This beach usually appeals more to travelers who are happy with simple scenery and space rather than food stalls, activity operators or a packed evening scene.

7. Sasawane Beach

Sasawane Beach is located near the ferry route connecting Mumbai and Mandwa. The beach offers beautiful sea views and fewer crowds compared to the main tourist beaches.

Because of its location, Sasawane can fit nicely into a ferry-based itinerary for visitors coming from Mumbai who want a quieter coastal stop before or after reaching the main Alibaug stay zone.

How to plan a hidden-beaches route

The easiest way to explore these beaches is to group them by direction rather than trying to see all of them in one trip. Kihim and Sasawane work better on a Mandwa-side route, while Awas, Varsoli and Akshi are easier to connect from the main Alibaug side. Revdanda fits better when you want a longer scenic coastal day.
