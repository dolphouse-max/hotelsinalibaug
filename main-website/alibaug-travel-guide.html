Alibaug Travel Guide – Beaches, Resorts and Things to Do

Alibaug is one of the most popular coastal destinations near Mumbai. Known for its clean beaches, historic forts and relaxed seaside atmosphere, the town attracts thousands of visitors every weekend. Many travelers visit Alibaug for beach holidays, water sports, seafood and peaceful resort stays.

Why Visit Alibaug?

Alibaug offers a perfect combination of beaches, historic attractions and relaxing coastal resorts. Because it is located close to Mumbai and Pune, it has become one of the most popular weekend destinations in Maharashtra.

Visitors come here to enjoy peaceful beach walks, fresh seafood, water sports and short coastal road trips. Whether you are planning a romantic getaway, a family vacation or a quick weekend trip, Alibaug offers plenty of options for accommodation and activities.

Places to Visit in Alibaug

Alibaug has several scenic beaches and historic attractions that make it one of the best coastal destinations in Maharashtra.

  • Nagaon Beach – Popular beach known for water sports and resorts.
  • Kashid Beach – White sand beach famous for scenic views.
  • Alibaug Beach – Located near the town and offering views of Kolaba Fort.
  • Kihim Beach – Quiet beach surrounded by greenery.
  • Kolaba Fort – Historic sea fort accessible during low tide.

Best Beaches in Alibaug

Alibaug is known for its beautiful coastline and several scenic beaches. Each beach has its own atmosphere and attractions.

  • Nagaon Beach – Water sports and beach resorts.
  • Kashid Beach – Famous for white sand and clear water.
  • Alibaug Beach – Known for views of Kolaba Fort.
  • Kihim Beach – Peaceful beach surrounded by greenery.

Where to Stay in Alibaug

Alibaug offers many types of accommodation ranging from luxury villas and boutique resorts to budget hotels and homestays.

Travelers can choose from beach resorts, budget hotels, cottages, and homestays depending on whether they want Nagaon Beach activity, Kashid scenery, Alibaug town access, or a quieter coastal base.

Best Time to Visit Alibaug

The best time to visit Alibaug is between October and March when the weather is pleasant and ideal for beach activities.

Monsoon season between June and September also has its charm, with lush greenery and fewer crowds.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best beaches in Alibaug?

Nagaon Beach, Kashid Beach, Alibaug Beach and Kihim Beach are among the most popular beaches.

How can I reach Alibaug from Mumbai?

You can reach Alibaug by ferry from Mumbai to Mandwa Jetty or by road via Panvel.

Is Alibaug good for weekend trips?

Yes, Alibaug is one of the most popular weekend destinations for travelers from Mumbai and Pune.
