Weekend Stay in Alibaug

Alibaug is one of the most popular beach destinations near Mumbai for short weekend trips. Travelers visit the town to enjoy its scenic beaches, coastal resorts and relaxed Konkan atmosphere. A weekend stay in Alibaug offers the perfect opportunity to explore beaches, enjoy seafood and experience the calm environment of the Arabian Sea coastline.

Top Resorts for a Weekend Stay in Alibaug

Most visitors planning a weekend stay in Alibaug prefer resorts located close to beaches like Nagaon Beach, Alibaug Beach or Kashid Beach. These areas offer easy beach access, restaurants and water sports activities.

1 Dolphin House Beach Resort

Dolphin House Beach Resort is a peaceful resort located close to Nagaon Beach. The property offers comfortable rooms, fresh seafood dining and a relaxed environment perfect for couples and families planning a weekend stay in Alibaug.

2 Radisson Blu Resort & Spa Alibaug

Radisson Blu is one of the most luxurious resorts in Alibaug, offering spacious villas, swimming pools and spa facilities. It is a popular choice for travelers seeking a premium weekend stay.

3 Sanman Beach Resort

Sanman Beach Resort is located near Nagaon Beach and offers comfortable rooms and a swimming pool. Families and groups often choose this resort for weekend trips.

4 U Tropicana Alibaug

U Tropicana is a stylish boutique resort surrounded by greenery. The resort features elegant villas and modern amenities for travelers looking for a relaxing weekend getaway.

5 Outpost @ Alibaug Beach Resort

Outpost is a modern lifestyle resort popular among young travelers. It offers contemporary interiors and a vibrant atmosphere for weekend beach stays.

6 Sai Inn Beach Resort

Sai Inn Beach Resort is located close to Nagaon Beach and provides easy access to water sports and beach activities.

7 Paradise Villas & Resort

Paradise Villas offers spacious villas surrounded by greenery. It is suitable for family vacations and group weekend trips.

8 Casuarina Resort

Casuarina Resort provides cottages and garden surroundings for visitors looking for a calm weekend stay near the beach.

Summary

Alibaug is a perfect destination for short weekend holidays. The town offers several beach resorts ranging from luxury villas to affordable stays near the coast. Resorts near Nagaon Beach are particularly popular because of their proximity to the beach and water sports activities.

Why Choose Alibaug for a Weekend Trip

Because Alibaug is located only a few hours from Mumbai, it has become one of the most popular weekend destinations in Maharashtra. Travelers often visit the town for beach walks, seafood restaurants and relaxing resort stays.

The combination of coastal scenery, comfortable resorts and easy accessibility makes Alibaug an ideal place for short holidays and quick weekend escapes from the city.
