Best Family Resorts in Alibaug

Alibaug is one of the most popular beach destinations near Mumbai for family holidays. The town offers several comfortable beach resorts where families can relax, enjoy the coastal atmosphere and spend time together. Many resorts in Alibaug provide spacious rooms, swimming pools and easy access to nearby beaches.

Top Family Resorts in Alibaug

Families visiting Alibaug often prefer resorts located close to the beach with comfortable rooms and dining facilities. Resorts near Nagaon Beach are particularly popular because the beach offers water sports and a lively coastal atmosphere.

1 Dolphin House Beach Resort

Dolphin House Beach Resort is a peaceful family-friendly resort located close to Nagaon Beach. The property offers comfortable rooms, fresh seafood dining and a relaxed environment suitable for families visiting Alibaug for weekend holidays.

2 Radisson Blu Resort & Spa Alibaug

Radisson Blu is one of the most luxurious family resorts in Alibaug. The property features large villas, swimming pools and recreational facilities ideal for family vacations.

3 Sanman Beach Resort

Sanman Beach Resort offers comfortable accommodation with a swimming pool and spacious rooms. Families often choose this property because of its convenient location near Nagaon Beach.

4 U Tropicana Alibaug

U Tropicana is a boutique resort surrounded by greenery and offers elegant villas and modern facilities suitable for families looking for a premium holiday experience.

5 Paradise Villas & Resort

Paradise Villas offers spacious villas and garden surroundings making it suitable for families and groups looking for a calm environment near the coast.

6 Sai Inn Beach Resort

Sai Inn Beach Resort is a popular choice for families visiting Nagaon Beach. The property provides comfortable rooms and easy access to beach activities.

7 Casuarina Resort

Casuarina Resort offers cottages surrounded by greenery and provides a peaceful stay environment for family trips.

8 Outpost @ Alibaug Beach Resort

Outpost is a stylish modern resort that offers comfortable rooms and recreational areas suitable for families and groups.

Summary

Family resorts in Alibaug offer comfortable accommodation with amenities such as swimming pools, restaurants and easy beach access. Resorts near Nagaon Beach are especially popular because they provide a lively coastal atmosphere and water sports activities.
