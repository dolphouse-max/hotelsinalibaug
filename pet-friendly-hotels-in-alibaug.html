Pet Friendly Hotels in Alibaug

Traveling with pets has become increasingly popular, and many travelers look for hotels where they can bring their dogs or other pets along for the trip. Alibaug is a great destination for pet owners because of its open beaches, relaxed coastal environment and weekend-friendly resorts.

Several resorts in the Alibaug region offer pet-friendly accommodation where visitors can enjoy beach holidays without leaving their pets behind.

Top Pet Friendly Hotels in Alibaug

1 Dolphin House Beach Resort

Dolphin House Beach Resort is a peaceful stay option located near Nagaon Beach. The resort offers comfortable rooms and a relaxed coastal environment suitable for families, couples and travelers visiting with pets.

Guests staying here can enjoy beach walks, outdoor space and nearby coastal areas ideal for relaxed holidays.

2 Pet Friendly Beach Villas

Private villas are often the most convenient choice for travelers visiting with pets because they offer more outdoor space and privacy.

3 Boutique Pet Friendly Resorts

Some boutique resorts in Alibaug welcome pets and provide open garden areas where pets can move freely.

4 Family Style Homestays

Homestays are sometimes more flexible with pet policies and are a good option for longer stays.

5 Private Pool Villas

Pool villas are ideal for pet owners who want complete privacy and space for pets to roam.

6 Garden Resorts

Resorts with large garden areas are often more suitable for travelers bringing pets.

7 Beachside Cottages

Beach cottages provide relaxed coastal stays and easy beach access for morning walks with pets.

8 Pet Friendly Guest Houses

Guest houses and smaller properties sometimes offer flexible pet policies for weekend visitors.

Why Travelers Bring Pets to Alibaug

  • Open beach areas
  • Relaxed coastal atmosphere
