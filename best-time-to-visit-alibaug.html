Best Time to Visit Alibaug – Weather & Travel Tips

Alibaug is a popular beach destination located along the Konkan coast of Maharashtra. Travelers from Mumbai and Pune often visit the town for weekend trips, beach holidays and seafood dining experiences. Choosing the right time to visit can make your trip more comfortable and enjoyable.

Winter Season (October to March)

The winter season is generally considered the best time to visit Alibaug. During these months the weather is pleasant with cooler temperatures and clear skies.

Visitors can comfortably explore beaches, enjoy water sports and visit attractions such as Kolaba Fort. The pleasant weather also makes it a good time for beach walks and sunset viewing.

Monsoon Season (June to September)

During the monsoon season, Alibaug becomes lush and green. The coastal scenery looks beautiful with dramatic skies and waves hitting the shoreline.

However, some beach activities such as water sports may not operate during heavy rainfall. Travelers who enjoy scenic landscapes and peaceful trips may still find monsoon visits very enjoyable.

Summer Season (April to May)

Summers in Alibaug can be warm, but many travelers still visit the destination for short beach breaks.

During summer, it is best to plan outdoor activities during early mornings or evenings when temperatures are more comfortable. Beach sunsets and seafood dinners are popular activities during this time.

Where to Stay in Alibaug

When planning a trip to Alibaug, choosing accommodation close to the beach can enhance your travel experience. Many travelers prefer staying near Nagaon Beach because of its convenient location and scenic coastline.

Dolphin House Beach Resort is a comfortable stay option located near Nagaon Beach. The resort offers relaxing rooms and a peaceful environment suitable for couples, families and weekend travelers.
