Frequently Asked Questions about Alibaug Stays

What are the best beach resorts in Alibaug for families?

The best beach resorts in Alibaug for families include Dolphin House Beach Resort near Nagaon Beach and several luxury stays near Kashid. These areas offer clean sands, water sports, and family-friendly cottages.

Which is the cleanest beach in Alibaug to stay near?

Nagaon Beach and Kashid Beach are widely considered the cleanest beaches near Alibaug. They are famous for their white sand, cypress (suru) trees, and high-quality beach resorts.

How far is Alibaug from Mumbai for a weekend trip?

Alibaug is approximately 100 km by road from Mumbai. However, the fastest way is via the Ro-Ro Ferry from M2M Ferries (Ferry Wharf) to Mandwa, which takes about 60 minutes.

Are there budget hotels in Alibaug near the beach?

Yes, there are many budget-friendly hotels and homestays in Alibaug, particularly around Nagaon and Varsoli beach, offering affordable rates for weekend travelers.

Which Alibaug resort is best for water sports?

Resorts located near Nagaon Beach are best for water sports enthusiasts, as this beach serves as the primary hub for jet skiing, parasailing, and banana boat rides in Alibaug.

Why stay near Nagaon Beach?

Nagaon is famous for its clean cypress-lined sand and crystal-clear waters, making it the safest beach in Alibaug for swimming. It is the central hub for Alibaug water sports like parasailing, jet-skiing, and banana boat rides.
