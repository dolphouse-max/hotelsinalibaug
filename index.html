Why Visit Alibaug?

Alibaug is one of the most popular coastal destinations near Mumbai and Pune. Located around 100 km from Mumbai, the town is known for clean beaches, relaxing resorts, seafood restaurants and beautiful sunset views.

Visitors come here for beach holidays, water sports, seafood and short weekend trips along the Konkan coastline.
