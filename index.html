Why Visit Alibaug?

Alibaug is one of the most popular beach destinations near Mumbai and Pune. Located around 100 kilometers from Mumbai, the town offers clean beaches, relaxed coastal resorts and a peaceful environment perfect for weekend trips.

Visitors travel to Alibaug to enjoy beach sunsets, water sports, seafood restaurants and short coastal road trips. The destination is known for beautiful beaches like Nagaon Beach, Kashid Beach and Alibaug Beach.
