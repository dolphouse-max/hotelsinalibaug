Best Things to Do in Alibaug

Alibaug is one of the most popular beach destinations near Mumbai. The town is known for its relaxing beaches, historic forts, water sports and coastal resorts. Visitors often travel to Alibaug for weekend trips, enjoying beach sunsets, seafood restaurants and peaceful seaside stays.

Top Things to Do in Alibaug

From relaxing beach walks to water sports and historic landmarks, Alibaug offers plenty of activities for visitors. Below are some of the most popular experiences travelers enjoy when visiting this coastal destination.

1. Visit Nagaon Beach

Nagaon Beach is one of the most famous beaches in Alibaug. The wide sandy shoreline and coconut trees create a perfect setting for relaxing beach holidays. Visitors can also enjoy water sports such as jet ski rides and banana boat rides.

2. Explore Kolaba Fort

Kolaba Fort is one of the most historic attractions in Alibaug. Located just off the shore of Alibaug Beach, the fort can be reached by walking during low tide. The fort offers beautiful views of the Arabian Sea.

3. Enjoy Water Sports

Adventure lovers can try various water sports activities in Alibaug, especially at Nagaon Beach. Popular activities include jet skiing, parasailing and banana boat rides.

4. Relax at Kashid Beach

Kashid Beach is known for its white sand and clear water. It is one of the most scenic beaches near Alibaug and perfect for relaxing coastal trips.

5. Try Local Seafood

Alibaug is famous for its coastal cuisine. Visitors often enjoy fresh seafood dishes such as fish fry, prawn curry and crab masala served in local restaurants and beach resorts.

6. Watch Sunset at Alibaug Beach

Sunset views in Alibaug are beautiful and peaceful. Many visitors gather at the beach in the evening to watch the sun set over the Arabian Sea.

7. Visit Kihim Beach

Kihim Beach is a quieter beach surrounded by greenery. It is ideal for travelers who prefer peaceful coastal locations away from crowds.

8. Explore Coastal Villages

The region around Alibaug includes several traditional Konkan villages. Exploring these villages offers visitors a glimpse of local culture and coastal life.

9. Take a Ferry Ride from Mumbai

One of the most enjoyable parts of visiting Alibaug is the ferry journey from Mumbai to Mandwa Jetty. The ferry ride offers scenic sea views and takes about 45–60 minutes.

10. Stay at a Beach Resort

Many travelers choose to stay at beach resorts in Alibaug to enjoy sea views and a relaxing environment. Resorts near Nagaon Beach are especially popular for weekend stays.
