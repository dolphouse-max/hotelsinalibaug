Best Hotels Near Alibaug Beach

Alibaug Beach is one of the most visited coastal locations in Maharashtra. Many travelers prefer staying in hotels close to the beach so they can enjoy sunset views, coastal walks and easy access to nearby restaurants and water sports activities.

Top Hotels Near Alibaug Beach

1 Dolphin House Beach Resort

Dolphin House Beach Resort is a peaceful coastal stay located near Nagaon Beach in the Alibaug region. The resort offers comfortable rooms and a relaxed environment suitable for couples, families and weekend travelers.

Guests can enjoy beach walks, seafood dining and easy access to nearby beaches during their stay.

2 Radisson Blu Resort & Spa Alibaug

Radisson Blu is one of the most luxurious resorts in the Alibaug region. It offers spacious villas, spa facilities, swimming pools and landscaped gardens.

3 Sanman Beach Resort

Sanman Beach Resort located near Nagaon Beach provides comfortable accommodation with convenient beach access.

4 U Tropicana Alibaug

U Tropicana is a boutique resort surrounded by greenery. It offers elegant villas and a peaceful environment ideal for relaxing holidays.

5 Outpost @ Alibaug Beach Resort

Outpost is a stylish modern resort popular among young travelers and couples visiting Alibaug for weekend trips.

6 Sai Inn Beach Resort

Sai Inn Beach Resort offers convenient access to Nagaon Beach and nearby water sports activities.

7 Paradise Villas & Resort

Paradise Villas offers spacious accommodation and garden surroundings for families and groups visiting Alibaug.

8 Casuarina Resort Alibaug

Casuarina Resort is a peaceful property surrounded by trees and greenery offering comfortable coastal stays.
