Best Resorts Near Nagaon Beach in Alibaug

Nagaon Beach is one of the most popular beaches in Alibaug and a favorite destination for weekend travelers from Mumbai. The area is known for its wide sandy shoreline, water sports activities and relaxing beach resorts. Many visitors prefer staying in resorts located close to the beach so they can easily enjoy beach walks, sunset views and local seafood restaurants.
