Best Resorts Near Nagaon Beach in Alibaug

Nagaon Beach is one of the most popular beaches in Alibaug, known for its clean shoreline, water sports and relaxed coastal atmosphere. Many travelers from Mumbai and Pune visit Nagaon for weekend trips and prefer staying at resorts close to the beach. Below is a curated list of some of the best resorts near Nagaon Beach for a comfortable stay.

Top 8 Resorts Near Nagaon Beach

1 Dolphin House Beach Resort

Dolphin House Beach Resort is a peaceful coastal stay located close to Nagaon Beach. The resort offers comfortable rooms, a relaxed environment and a restaurant serving fresh seafood and local dishes. It is ideal for couples, families and small groups looking for a calm beachside weekend getaway in Alibaug.

Visit Website

2 Sanman Beach Resort

Sanman Beach Resort is a popular resort located very close to Nagaon Beach. The property offers spacious rooms, a swimming pool and convenient beach access, making it a good choice for family holidays and weekend travelers.

3 Sai Inn Beach Resort

Sai Inn Beach Resort is known for its comfortable rooms and proximity to water sports activities on Nagaon Beach. The resort is often chosen by tourists looking for a simple and convenient beach stay.

4 Paradise Villas & Resort

Paradise Villas offers spacious accommodations surrounded by greenery. The resort provides a peaceful environment and is located a short distance from Nagaon Beach.

5 Outpost @ Alibaug Beach Resort

Outpost is a modern boutique resort known for its stylish interiors and relaxed atmosphere. It is popular among young travelers and couples visiting Alibaug for short stays.

6 Ruturaj Inn Beach Resort

Ruturaj Inn Beach Resort offers comfortable accommodation and a calm environment for visitors who want to stay near Nagaon Beach without the crowds of larger resorts.

7 Sea Castle Beach Resort

Sea Castle Beach Resort is located close to the beach and offers basic but comfortable accommodation for travelers looking for a budget-friendly beach resort stay.

8 Neel Garden Resort

Neel Garden Resort is surrounded by greenery and offers a relaxed environment for families and groups visiting Nagaon Beach. The property provides comfortable rooms and open garden spaces.

Summary

Nagaon Beach is one of the best places to stay in Alibaug because of its beautiful coastline and water sports activities. Dolphin House Beach Resort is a popular option for travelers who want a peaceful stay near the beach with comfortable rooms and fresh seafood.
