Top 10 Best Hotels in Alibaug

Alibaug offers a wide variety of hotels and beach resorts ranging from luxury villas to family-friendly coastal stays. Many travelers visit Alibaug for short weekend trips from Mumbai and prefer hotels located near popular beaches like Nagaon Beach, Alibaug Beach and Kashid Beach.

1 Dolphin House Beach Resort

Dolphin House Beach Resort is a peaceful coastal resort located close to Nagaon Beach. The property offers comfortable rooms, a relaxed beachside environment and a restaurant serving fresh seafood and local cuisine. It is suitable for couples, families and small groups looking for a calm weekend getaway in Alibaug.

2 Radisson Blu Resort & Spa Alibaug

Radisson Blu is one of the most luxurious resorts in Alibaug, offering premium villas, large swimming pools and spa facilities. It is a popular choice for travelers seeking a luxury beach vacation with modern amenities.

3 Sanman Beach Resort

Sanman Beach Resort is located near Nagaon Beach and is known for its comfortable rooms, swimming pool and convenient beach access. It is a good option for families and groups visiting Alibaug for a weekend stay.

4 U Tropicana Alibaug

U Tropicana is a stylish boutique resort surrounded by greenery. The resort features luxury villas, elegant interiors and a peaceful environment ideal for relaxing holidays.

5 Outpost @ Alibaug Beach Resort

Outpost is a modern lifestyle resort popular among young travelers and couples. The property offers contemporary rooms and a vibrant atmosphere for weekend stays.

6 Sai Inn Beach Resort

Sai Inn Beach Resort is located close to Nagaon Beach and offers convenient access to water sports activities. It is often chosen by visitors looking for a simple and comfortable beach stay.

7 Paradise Villas & Resort

Paradise Villas offers spacious villas and garden surroundings. The resort is popular among families and groups looking for a peaceful environment close to the Alibaug coastline.

8 Casuarina Resort Alibaug

Casuarina Resort is known for its cottages and green surroundings. The property offers a relaxed stay experience and easy access to nearby beaches in the Alibaug region.

9 Neel Garden Resort

Neel Garden Resort is a calm property surrounded by trees and open spaces. It offers comfortable accommodation for families and small groups visiting Alibaug.

10 Sea Castle Beach Resort

Sea Castle Beach Resort offers affordable accommodation near the coast and is a good option for travelers looking for a budget beach hotel stay in Alibaug.
