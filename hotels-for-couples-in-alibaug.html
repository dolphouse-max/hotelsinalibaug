Romantic Resorts in Alibaug for Couples

Alibaug is one of the most popular weekend destinations for couples travelling from Mumbai and Pune. With scenic beaches, peaceful resorts and beautiful sunset views, the destination offers the perfect setting for romantic getaways and short coastal holidays.

Top Hotels for Couples in Alibaug

1 Dolphin House Beach Resort

Dolphin House Beach Resort is a peaceful stay option near Nagaon Beach. The resort offers comfortable rooms and a relaxed coastal atmosphere ideal for couples looking for a quiet beach getaway.

Couples staying here can enjoy beach walks, sunset views and seafood dinners during their visit.

Visit Website

2 Radisson Blu Resort & Spa Alibaug

Radisson Blu offers luxury villas, spa facilities and beautiful landscaped surroundings. It is a popular choice for couples looking for a premium romantic stay.

3 U Tropicana Alibaug

U Tropicana is known for its elegant villas and peaceful environment. The resort provides a comfortable setting for couples seeking privacy and relaxation.

4 Sanman Beach Resort

Sanman Beach Resort located near Nagaon Beach offers comfortable rooms and easy beach access, making it a convenient stay option for couples.

5 Outpost @ Alibaug Beach Resort

Outpost is a stylish resort popular among young travelers and couples who enjoy modern interiors and relaxed weekend stays.

6 Casuarina Resort Alibaug

Casuarina Resort offers cottages surrounded by greenery and provides a peaceful environment suitable for romantic vacations.

7 Sai Inn Beach Resort

Sai Inn Beach Resort is located close to Nagaon Beach and offers comfortable rooms with easy access to beach activities.

8 Paradise Villas & Resort

Paradise Villas offers spacious villas and garden surroundings suitable for couples looking for a relaxed coastal stay.
