2 Day Alibaug Itinerary for a Perfect Beach Weekend

Alibaug is one of the most popular weekend destinations near Mumbai. With its scenic beaches, historic forts and coastal cuisine, the town offers a perfect short holiday experience. This two day itinerary helps travelers explore the best of Alibaug while still keeping the trip relaxed and enjoyable.

Two days is usually the ideal format for Alibaug because it gives you enough time to combine beach relaxation, local food and one or two sightseeing stops without turning the trip into a rushed checklist.

Day 1 – Arrival and Beach Time

Start your Alibaug trip by travelling from Mumbai either by ferry or by road. Many visitors prefer taking the ferry from Gateway of India to Mandwa Jetty, which takes around one hour and offers beautiful views of the Arabian Sea.

If your goal is a smooth weekend rather than maximum sightseeing, it is usually better to pick one stay area, check in early and build the itinerary around that base instead of changing zones repeatedly.

Morning

  • Travel from Mumbai to Alibaug by ferry or road
  • Check into your beach resort or hotel
  • Head to Nagaon Beach for a relaxed beach walk

Afternoon

  • Enjoy water sports such as jet skiing or banana boat rides
  • Have lunch at a beachside restaurant
  • Relax at your resort or explore nearby beaches

Nagaon Beach is often the easiest first-day choice because it lets you combine arrival, beach time and dinner without spending too much extra time on local travel.

Evening Seafood Dinner

One of the highlights of visiting Alibaug is its seafood. Many coastal restaurants serve fresh fish, prawns and crab prepared in traditional Konkan style.

After spending the day at the beach, enjoy a relaxed seafood dinner at a local restaurant or at your resort.

This is usually the best time to slow the itinerary down. Alibaug works better as a relaxed coastal break than as a trip packed with constant movement.

Day 2 – Sightseeing and Coastal Views

The second day of your trip can be spent exploring historic attractions and enjoying more beach time before returning to Mumbai.

Most travelers do better on day two by choosing either a fort-and-town plan or a scenic-beach extension rather than trying to do everything before the return journey.

Morning

  • Visit Kolaba Fort near Alibaug Beach
  • Take photographs of the fort and coastline
  • Enjoy breakfast at a local cafe

Afternoon

  • Visit Kashid Beach or Kihim Beach
  • Relax by the sea and enjoy the coastal views
  • Return to Mumbai by evening

Where to Stay in Alibaug

For a comfortable weekend trip, staying near the beach is often the best option. Many resorts are located near Nagaon Beach and offer quick access to the coastline.

Dolphin House Beach Resort is a popular stay option for travelers visiting Alibaug. The resort offers comfortable rooms and a relaxed coastal environment suitable for couples and families.

Who this two-day itinerary suits best

  • Couples planning a balanced beach-and-food weekend
  • Families who want a slower short break
  • First-time visitors who want the easiest Alibaug format
  • Travelers from Mumbai who want enough time without taking extra leave

How to make a 2 day Alibaug trip feel less rushed

Pick one main beach zone, keep only one major sightseeing stop, and let the rest of the trip revolve around beach time, seafood and a comfortable stay. In Alibaug, a simpler itinerary usually feels better than an overpacked one.
