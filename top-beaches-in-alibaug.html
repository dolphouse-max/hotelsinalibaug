10 Best Beaches in Alibaug You Must Visit

Alibaug is one of the most popular coastal destinations near Mumbai. The region is known for its scenic beaches, water sports activities and relaxing seaside atmosphere.

Visitors traveling to Alibaug often explore multiple beaches during their trip. Some beaches are famous for water sports while others are known for peaceful coastal scenery and sunset views.

Nagaon Beach

Nagaon Beach is one of the most popular beaches in Alibaug. It is known for water sports activities such as jet skiing, banana boat rides and parasailing.

Kashid Beach

Kashid Beach is famous for its white sand shoreline and clear blue water. It is one of the most scenic beaches in the Alibaug region.

Alibaug Beach

Alibaug Beach is located close to the town center and offers beautiful views of Kolaba Fort. It is one of the most accessible beaches for visitors.

Kihim Beach

Kihim Beach is surrounded by greenery and is known for its calm and peaceful atmosphere. It is a popular destination for relaxing coastal walks.

Varsoli Beach

Varsoli Beach is located near Alibaug town and is known for its clean shoreline and peaceful environment.

Awas Beach

Awas Beach is a quieter beach located close to Alibaug town and is known for its scenic coastal surroundings.

Mandwa Beach

Mandwa Beach is famous for ferry access from Mumbai and beautiful sunset views over the Arabian Sea.

Revdanda Beach

Revdanda Beach is known for its historic Portuguese fort ruins and long quiet shoreline.

Akshi Beach

Akshi Beach is a peaceful destination with fewer crowds compared to other popular Alibaug beaches.

Murud Beach

Murud Beach is famous for the nearby Murud Janjira Fort and beautiful coastal scenery.

Where to Stay in Alibaug

Many travelers visiting these beaches prefer staying in nearby resorts so they can easily explore multiple locations.

Dolphin House Beach Resort is a comfortable stay option near Nagaon Beach and is suitable for couples, families and weekend travelers.
