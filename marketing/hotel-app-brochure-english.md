# Hotels In Alibaug Guest Check-In App

## Simple digital system for hotels, resorts, lodges and homestays

The Hotels In Alibaug Guest Check-In App helps hotel owners manage guest records, staff details, reservations, and reports from one mobile-friendly system.

## Key benefits

- QR guest self check-in for faster reception work
- Digital guest register with family member entry
- Staff register with role and contact details
- Future reservation calendar
- Hotel reports for current guests, register, and occupancy
- Hotel-owned proof storage in the hotel's connected Google Drive
- Controlled police-ready access through the app

## Why owners like it

- Less manual writing
- Faster front desk process
- Better record organization
- Easier guest and staff tracking
- More professional hotel operations

## Security and control

- Guest proof images stay in the hotel's own Google Drive
- Records remain hotel-owned
- Police and superadmin access is controlled inside the app
- No open browsing access to hotel proof storage
