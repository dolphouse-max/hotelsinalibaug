Security and control

Hotel-Owned Records

Guest proof images stay in the hotel’s connected Google Drive so the hotel keeps ownership of its data.

Controlled Police Access

Police users can review structured guest information through controlled in-app access.

No Open Browsing

Proof review does not mean open browsing of the hotel’s Google Drive storage.
