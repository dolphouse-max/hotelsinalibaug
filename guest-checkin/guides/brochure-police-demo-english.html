Hotels In Alibaug Police Access System

A structured, mobile-friendly police access workflow for hotel guest records, current occupancy, guest history, staff verification, and audited proof review.

Open Police Help Book Demo

What police can do

  • Search guest data across all hotels
  • Search by guest name, mobile number, or car number
  • Check hotel-wise current guests
  • Open guest history for selected dates
  • Review hotel staff records
  • See access logs for audit checking

Why this is useful

  • Faster than manual register checking
  • One mobile screen instead of multiple paper books
  • Quick hotel-wise or cross-hotel verification
  • Controlled proof access inside the app
  • Officer-name based usage trail
  • Useful during enquiry, surprise checks, and routine verification

Security and control

Controlled Police Login

Access is given only through police users created by superadmin.

Hotel-Owned Data

Hotels keep ownership of their records. The system presents police-ready data without giving open access to hotel storage.

Audited Access

Police Console can log who accessed which guest record and when.

No Open Browsing

Proof review is controlled inside the app and does not expose open browsing of hotel Google Drive.
