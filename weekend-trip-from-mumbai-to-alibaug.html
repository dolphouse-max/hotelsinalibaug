Perfect Weekend Trip from Mumbai to Alibaug

Alibaug is one of the most popular weekend getaway destinations near Mumbai. Located along the Konkan coast of Maharashtra, the town is known for its beautiful beaches, coastal resorts and relaxed atmosphere. Thousands of travelers visit Alibaug every weekend to enjoy beach sunsets, seafood restaurants and short coastal road trips.

Distance from Mumbai to Alibaug

The distance between Mumbai and Alibaug is approximately 95–110 kilometers depending on the route taken. Travel time usually ranges from 2.5 to 3 hours by road. Many travelers prefer using the ferry from Mumbai to Mandwa because it reduces travel time significantly.

Alibaug has become a favorite weekend destination for Mumbai residents because it offers beaches, water sports, seafood restaurants and many resorts suitable for short coastal holidays.

How to Reach Alibaug from Mumbai

1. Ferry from Mumbai to Mandwa

The ferry service from Mumbai to Mandwa Jetty is the fastest and most popular way to reach Alibaug. Ferries operate from the Gateway of India and Bhaucha Dhakka ferry terminals.

The ferry journey takes about 45–60 minutes. After reaching Mandwa, buses and taxis are available to travel to Alibaug town and nearby beaches.

2. Road Trip from Mumbai

Driving from Mumbai to Alibaug is another great option for a weekend trip. The road journey offers scenic views of coastal villages and countryside. Popular driving routes include the Mumbai–Panvel–Pen–Alibaug highway.

Best Beaches to Visit During a Weekend Trip

  • Nagaon Beach – Popular for water sports and beach resorts
  • Alibaug Beach – Known for views of Kolaba Fort
  • Kashid Beach – Famous for its white sand and clear water
  • Kihim Beach – Quiet beach surrounded by greenery

Among these, Nagaon Beach is one of the most visited beaches because of its clean shoreline, water sports activities and resort options.

Suggested 2 Day Weekend Itinerary

Day 1

  • Morning ferry or drive from Mumbai
  • Check in at a beach resort
  • Visit Nagaon Beach for water sports
  • Enjoy seafood dinner at a coastal restaurant

Day 2

  • Morning walk at Alibaug Beach
  • Visit Kolaba Fort
  • Explore nearby beaches like Kashid or Kihim
  • Return to Mumbai by evening

How long does it take to travel from Mumbai to Alibaug?

Is Alibaug good for a one night trip?
